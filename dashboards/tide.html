How to read this

Live and backtest, labelled

Live is Tide on our own account since 8 October 2026: every ladder it opens, at the prices it filled, after fees and funding. The return counts the ladders still open at today's price, so it moves with the market between sells. Its growth is each step's profit or loss divided by the capital Tide was sizing from at the time, so deposits do not count as gains.

The backtest runs the same rules over the coins and large stocks Hyperliquid lists, November 2023 to October 2026, after fees and funding, with the coins that later collapsed left in. It picks each month's markets by trading volume, as the live bot does. Its chart uses month-end values, so its drawdown there looks shallower than the −22% the full record reached between month ends.

Neither is your account's result: your size and timing decide that. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
