How the vault is run

Elite is a discretionary vault managed by the founder, who trades it by hand on the vault's behalf. A few small systematic positions run alongside. Markets, position sizes and leverage change at the manager's discretion, and the book can be concentrated.

Depositors share the vault's gains and losses in proportion to their stake. The manager takes 10% of profits and keeps his own money in the vault. Leveraged perpetual futures can lose a large share of their value quickly; past results do not predict future ones.
