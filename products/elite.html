HOW IT IS RUN

Automation runs alongside.

HONEST LIMITS

What Elite isn't

Discretionary, not systematic

Most of the vault is traded by hand. Results depend on one person's judgement, and no backtest describes what the vault will do.

Leveraged perpetuals

The vault trades leveraged perpetual futures and can hold concentrated positions. Losses are amplified and can be large and fast. Check the drawdown on the live dashboard before you deposit.

Not a balanced portfolio

Markets, direction and size change with the manager's view. Elite is not a hedge, an index or a market-neutral product.

Past results don't carry forward

The dashboard documents what the vault has done, not what it will do.
