HOW IT WORKS

Three things, every time

1
Waits for a real dip

Each month Tide trades the coins people trade most, plus 17 large stocks such as NVIDIA, TSMC and Tesla. It acts only when one is properly oversold, on completed candles. Most of the time most of your money sits idle, waiting.

2
Buys in steps

The first buy is small. If the price keeps falling, Tide buys more at lower prices, so the average price follows the dip down and a smaller bounce is enough to win.

3
Sells the bounce

The whole ladder closes at a profit target above its average price, usually within a few days. A stop rests under every ladder, so one that never bounces is closed instead of held forever.

SETUP · ABOUT 10 MINUTES

Your account, your keys

  1. Give Tide an account of its own. It sizes from the whole balance and trades most coins, so it cannot share with another bot. A Hyperliquid sub-account works, or a new wallet. Switch it to Unified account so it can trade the stocks too.
  2. Subscribe on Whop and pick your plan.
  3. Create an API wallet at app.hyperliquid.xyz/API. It can trade but can never withdraw, and you can revoke it there at any time.
  4. Connect it on the Soomario engine and pick Tide. On a sub-account, the engine offers it straight from your main wallet's card, no new key needed.

If you pause or your plan lapses, Tide opens no new ladders but keeps managing the open ones until each reaches its target or its stop. How the engine holds keys →

HONEST LIMITS

What a 99% win rate hides

Many small wins, a few big losses

Almost every ladder ends at its target for a small gain. The rare one that keeps falling ends at its stop, and that costs about 8% of the account, up to about 12% when a coin collapses straight through it. A year of small wins can give back a month's worth in one bad week.

It only buys

Tide never shorts. In a market that keeps falling it mostly waits, and that is when its stops happen: May 2022 was the worst month in every test.

The backtest is the optimistic end

It has no survivorship bias, but it fills at modelled prices, and the stocks have only traded on Hyperliquid since 2023. The live record is weeks old. Every live ladder is published next to the backtest, never mixed into it, so you can watch the two side by side.
