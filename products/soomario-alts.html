HONEST LIMITS

Read this before you deposit

Your collateral is what's at risk

At the first level the drawdown limit equals your deposit exactly: if the account loses 20%, it closes and the $100 (or $500) is gone. Foxify funds the rest, so you can't lose more than you put down, but you can lose all of it.

Many small wins, fewer bigger losses

Most trades close with a small profit and the losers are larger, so a run of losers in a bad week can take an account to its limit. Several trades in the same direction can be open at once, which makes a sharp market move hit harder.

New, and in beta

Soomario Alts has traded on Foxify since October 2026 and Foxify Automation is still in beta. We will publish its record once it has a few months behind it. Until then, start with an amount you are ready to lose.

Foxify's costs and rules apply

Trading fees, funding and any fee for a drawdown reset are Foxify's, as are the account rules, which they can change. Their documentation has them in full.
