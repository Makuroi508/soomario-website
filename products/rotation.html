THE EDGE

Conviction first,
consensus second

Most systematic equity books chase momentum or analyst consensus. Rotation deliberately overweights long-horizon conviction, then uses consensus and technicals as secondary confirmation - with a dip bonus and an extension penalty so a vertical name cannot sit at the top. The result is a concentrated, high-conviction book that compounds a Tesla core while rotating a small slate of the highest-scoring names. It is an active book: membership is checked throughout the day and weights are rebalanced regularly.

HOW IT WORKS

Two roles for capital

PERMANENT CORE
Tesla core

Tesla is held as a permanent core, separate from the rotation and not scored. It also acts as a ratchet: every rotation trim routes a fixed share of proceeds into Tesla, so the core compounds each time a rotation winner is skimmed - up to a ceiling allocation. The core takes profit in stages after strong runs, and a trailing take-profit harvests the remainder.

Permanent · ratchets up via the skim · capped at ceiling
SCORED ROTATION
Fastest Horse rotation

A small number of rotating slots are filled by the highest-scoring names from a curated mega-cap universe - household large-cap, semiconductor, cloud, and crypto-adjacent leaders. Selection uses a composite score that overweights long-horizon conviction, with a buffer that protects incumbents to prevent churn and only one swap per cycle.

Composite-scored · churn-protected · checked through the day
THE FASTEST HORSE SCORE

How names are ranked

Each candidate is scored across three layers plus adjustments; the exact weights are private. Visionary and consensus upside are normalized across the universe, so the score reflects relative upside rather than an absolute number.

Visionary upside · highest weight

Long-horizon conviction price targets (ARK / InvestAnswers style), operator-set and version-tracked since August 2026. The largest share of the score.

Wall Street consensus · secondary

Mean analyst target, refreshed regularly from public data. A smaller share of the score: confirmation, not the primary driver.

Technical momentum · secondary

A blend of standard momentum and trend readings that times entries. A smaller share of the score, alongside consensus.

Dip bonus & extension penalty

A small positive adjustment rewards a name trading at a discount to its trend while conviction stays high; an extension penalty docks names that are overbought or far above trend, so a vertical name cannot sit at the top.

MECHANICS

Rotation, dip buys, rebalancing

Rotation & churn control

The top names by composite fill the slots. A buffer protects incumbents, so a benched name must beat the weakest holder by a meaningful margin to displace it, and only one swap happens per cycle. When a rotation occurs the outgoing name is closed and banked; nothing is averaged into the new name.

Buying the dips in steps

Every position buys in steps on dips. The first buy fills at entry and further buys fill on pullbacks, front-loading conviction into the first buy and keeping later ones light. The steps are sized so reserve capital is put to work in a normal correction.

Rebalancer

A rebalancer runs on a regular schedule. Each slot has a target weight proportional to its composite and a tolerance around it; within tolerance it does nothing. Overweights are trimmed, feeding underweight laggards and the Tesla skim. A trend gate blocks adds to names still falling.

VENUE, EXECUTION & CUSTODY

Solana spot, self-custodied

Venue

The strategy trades on Solana mainnet - the live and only venue. Positions are tokenized US equities held as spot tokens in one Solana wallet, priced and quoted in USDC. Two instrument families are used: Backed Finance xStocks (Token-2022) and Ondo-wrapped equities where no xStock exists. There are no perpetual contracts and no leverage.

Execution

All swaps route through the Jupiter aggregator using its Ultra endpoints - the same routing the public Jupiter interface uses. Ultra aggregates RFQ market makers and on-chain AMM liquidity and manages slippage dynamically, so a thin name that fails on a narrow fixed-slippage endpoint can still be quoted through Ultra's broader coverage.

Custody

Positions are real tokens self-custodied in a single Solana wallet, priced in USDC. There is no exchange account and no margin. The internal book is reconciled against actual wallet balances on a schedule, and true wallet value is snapshotted at live Jupiter marks.

RISK FRAMEWORK

Discipline in the code

No leverage

Spot only - no margin, no liquidation. The strategy's market risk is the spot price of the held tokens. The live book is 100% long across the Tesla core and the rotation; there are no short sleeves.

Zone-aware stops

A zone-aware stop sits below the deepest dip buy, giving the steps room to fill before any exit. A cooldown blacklists a name after it is stopped out to prevent immediate re-entry.

Order-fail safety

Opens, reduces, and closes route to the venue first and abort without writing a phantom fill if the venue rejects the order - a fix introduced after a book-corruption incident.

Price-impact guard

Every order's price impact is checked before signing and rejected if it exceeds a configured ceiling. On an unlevered spot book, this guard is the primary protection against poor fills.

Around-the-clock execution

Tokenized stocks trade 24/7, and the live book currently executes rotations, dip buys and stops at any hour, weekends included, where off-hours spreads can be wider. Quote retries are confined to the pre-signing step, so a retry cannot double-execute.

Kill switch & alerts

A halt can stop new entries while leaving take-profit, stop-loss, and trailing exits active. A worker heartbeat surfaces a frozen process, and categorized Telegram alerts cover fills, rejects, and failures.

HONEST LIMITS

What Rotation isn't

Concentration

The book concentrates in a Tesla core and a small stock rotation. This is deliberate, but it means idiosyncratic single-name risk is high.

Tokenized-equity risk

Tokenized equities carry issuer, custody, smart-contract, and on-chain liquidity risk distinct from a traditional brokerage. A thin name can be untradeable automatically at some moments even when it is quotable by hand; such a name is diagnosed and, if it has no reliable on-chain route at the strategy's sizes, held manually rather than forced.

Statistical, not deterministic

The scoring and rebalancing are probabilistic edges, not guarantees. Frequent trims and dip buys mean trading costs and taxable events add up.

Liquidity pools

A small slice of each position (about 8%) is placed in Raydium concentrated-liquidity ranges to earn swap fees. Those ranges sell into strength and buy into weakness, and the fees are reported before that cost.

Early track record

Win rate and realized P&L should be read with care while the system is young. Until a losing exit occurs, realized win rate reflects only profitable trims and is not a measure of strategy quality. Do not commit capital you cannot afford to lose.
