How we publish records

The platform whitepaper (v2.0) was archived in September 2026 and is no longer current. This page says where each bot's record comes from and where you can check it.

Hype Bot

Every signal published to subscribers since 6 November 2025, at the price each signal carried, charged a 0.12% round trip (the cost we measured on our own account). It is rebuilt once a month from the signal list. Before that date the record is the backtest it was built on, labelled as such. The Hype Bot record lists every trade and links the full CSV.

Tide

The live record is our own account since 8 October 2026, read from the bot as you load the page, with open ladders priced at the market. The backtest is published ladder by ladder next to it, never mixed into it. The Tide record shows both.

Accumulator

A model portfolio that has followed every signal since 9 February 2026, marked daily, with every buy on the public dashboard. It shows two returns: the time-weighted strategy return and your money's return on the dollars put in. The Accumulator dashboard.

None of these is your account's result: your exchange, size and timing decide that. Past performance does not guarantee future results. The exact strategy settings are private; every trade is published.
