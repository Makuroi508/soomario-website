BEFORE YOU TURN IT UP

What size and leverage do to this strategy

Exposure multiplies the losers too

Margin × leverage is the exposure, and exposure is what scales the record. At 100% every trade lands as published; at 180% every trade, the losing ones included, lands at 1.8 times. The Hype Bot wins often and small and loses rarely and large, so the 10% hard stop is an 18% loss of the account at 180% exposure and 27% at 270%. Look at the max drawdown above, not only at the final balance.

Leverage moves liquidation closer

Doubling the leverage with the same margin doubles every win and every loss, and halves the distance to liquidation: the price can go the whole way against you at 1x, about half at 2x, about a third at 3x. The hard stop rests on the exchange inside that distance, but a gap through the stop fills wherever the market is.

The record is the optimistic end

The tester fills at the bar's price with 0.2% commission and no slippage, and HYPE rose about 450% over this period. Run the same rules at one-minute resolution and the average trade is nearer +0.5% than +0.8%. Multiply your expectations down before you multiply the leverage up.
